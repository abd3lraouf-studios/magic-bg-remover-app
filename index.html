Frequently asked questions

Is Magic BG Remover free?
Yes — it's completely free with no watermark, no sign-up, and no usage limits.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. All background removal happens locally in your browser, so your images never leave your device.
Does the background remover work offline?
Yes. After your first visit it installs as a PWA and works fully offline, including the AI model.
What image formats can I export?
Transparent PNG, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL (JXL), and JPEG — each with quality control and a live file-size estimate.
How does the AI background removal work?
It runs an ISNet image-segmentation model via WebAssembly on your device to detect and cut out the subject — no cloud processing.
